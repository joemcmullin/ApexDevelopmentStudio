About the founder

Joe McMullin. Built on service.

Joe McMullinFounder & Principal · Aldie, Virginia

Joe McMullin is the Founder and Principal of Apex Development Studio LLC and a senior leader who runs cloud engineering and cybersecurity teams in secure government environments. He advises organizational leaders on technology, cybersecurity and operations, and leads transformation teams through large-scale infrastructure projects that modernize critical capabilities. His career spans 22 years of Army operations worldwide and two decades of federal and enterprise technology.

Career

Military operations

22 years in the U.S. Army. As a senior leader, he planned, coordinated and supervised security-critical operations at senior headquarters and joint commands.

He led worldwide missions across Central and South America, Asia and Europe, ran regional operations and trained others to run them. He brought new technologies into service, reworked operating protocols, and was selected for special planning projects. He was awarded the Bronze Star Medal and five Meritorious Service Medals.

Federal & enterprise technology

Two decades in federal and healthcare IT. His work has spanned bare-metal servers, virtualization and hybrid cloud. With a large federal systems integrator he delivered systems and infrastructure for federal and military agencies. With a large federal contractor he managed global healthcare software systems, infrastructure and support, cut response times on a global customer issue by 10%, and led engineers who earned government recognition for a record-breaking data-center recovery. He organized a global team on a $14M program that won Government CTO approval for a cloud architecture and migration plan, cutting infrastructure needs by 50%.

With a mid-sized federal contractor he led cybersecurity and hybrid-cloud infrastructure for two major federal agencies, including the U.S. Treasury's Financial Crimes Enforcement Network, across 24/7 cloud and geographically separated data centers. With a global consulting firm he led a large management team on a $285M enterprise IT contract for the Defense Counterintelligence and Security Agency. He has headed a $40M program moving secure workloads to Microsoft 365 GCC High and AWS GovCloud, and $25M+ in data-center and enterprise modernization. Today he leads the cloud engineering and cybersecurity teams behind ATOs on AWS GovCloud, Microsoft 365 GCC High, IL4 and IL5, including annual reviews, STIG hardening and continuous monitoring.

Advisor · Apex Development Studio

Founder of Apex Development Studio. Joe brings global military operations and two decades of federal technology leadership to the leaders he advises. He has directed strategy and operations for programs worth more than $100M, and helps organizations modernize at scale, protect what they run, and keep operations steady through change.

He pairs that field experience with business strategy training from Cornell University, and leads transformation teams through large-scale infrastructure projects and capability upgrades. At HINA he leads ALPHA, the knowledge arm, and runs operations and technology.
