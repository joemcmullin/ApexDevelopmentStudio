How every project runs

One process, every platform.

  1. Discovery

    We talk through the problem, the people it's for, and what success looks like. You get a written scope and estimate.

  2. Design and prototype

    Screens and flows you can tap through before any production code is written.

  3. Build with quality gates

    Every feature passes code review, automated tests, an accessibility check and a privacy check before it's done.

  4. Launch

    App Store and Google Play submission, or the website going live on your domain, handled for you.

  5. Support

    Fixes, updates for new OS versions, and new features after launch.
