App Review

We know what Apple will ask.

Most app delays happen at App Review, not in development. Health apps get the closest look of all. We've taken a HealthKit app that uses clinical health records through review, and every submission now goes out with the review notes, privacy manifest, purpose strings and data-use answers already prepared.

  • Privacy manifests and nutrition labels that match what the code actually does
  • Clear review notes and demo access, so reviewers don't have to guess
  • No medical claims in app copy or screenshots
  • Calm, specific replies when App Review has questions

Questions

Before you ask.

Do you also build the Android version?

Yes. We build Android natively in Kotlin and Jetpack Compose and keep it in step with the iOS app, so both feel right on their platform. See Android app development.

Can you take over or improve an existing iOS app?

Yes. We start with a code and App Store review audit, then fix what's risky before adding features.

Do you handle App Store submission?

Yes: screenshots, metadata, privacy labels, review notes and responses to App Review, through to approval.

Can you build apps that use health data?

Yes. Journey Tracker reads and writes HealthKit data, including clinical health records, and passed App Review with them. Health data stays on the person's devices.

How much does an app cost?

It depends on scope. After a first conversation we send a written scope and estimate, so you know the cost before work starts.
