Questions

Before you ask.

Do you write the content, or do we?

We can do either. Most projects include research and copywriting, because a website is only as good as what it says.

Who hosts the site?

Whatever suits you: static hosting, your existing provider, or a platform you already pay for. We set up the domain and HTTPS.

Can you build a site for an app that isn't launched yet?

Yes. We build pre-launch sites with a waitlist, then switch them to App Store and Google Play download pages at launch.

Will the site be accessible and fast on phones?

Yes. Every page is checked for accessibility and mobile speed before launch. This site scores 99 for mobile performance and 100 for accessibility and SEO in Google Lighthouse.
